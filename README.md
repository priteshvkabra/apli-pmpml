# Apli PMPML: getting app holders to book in the app

A working prototype for **Apli PMPML**, the city bus app for Pune and Pimpri-Chinchwad.

> Independent product case study. Not affiliated with PMPML or its app vendor.

## The problem

Riders who have the app still buy from the conductor. With the conductor, they say one stop name. In the app, they fill a blank English form for the same trip, every time.

## What changes, in order of priority

| Priority | Change | What the rider gets |
|---|---|---|
| P0 | **Book again** | Usual trips on Home, from their own tickets. Tap the bus you're on, then pay. |
| P1 | **Stop search by everyday names** | "station", "रेल्वे" or "kharadi" finds the stop, in any script. |
| P1 | **Help the first time** | One short tip on each box of the real form, on the first booking. |
| P2 | **Full Marathi and Hindi** | Language chosen once. Every screen follows it, stop names included. |
| P2 | **Pass before the first bus** | When the rider's usual day costs more than ₹70, Home says so before the first bus. |

## Files

- `index.html`: the prototype. Pick a rider type and tap anything on the phone.
- `walkthrough.html`: a guided, step-by-step walkthrough with notes, and today's app compared with the new screens.
